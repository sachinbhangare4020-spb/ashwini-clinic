Precautions & Viral Protection

Simple habits that keep you and your family safe.

Prevention

How to Stay Safe from Viral Infections

  • Wash hands for 20 seconds
  • Cover your mouth when coughing
  • Avoid crowded places during outbreaks
  • Stay home when you have fever
Monsoon

Dengue, Malaria and Flu: What to Do

  • Remove stagnant water around home
  • Use mosquito nets and repellents
  • Drink boiled or filtered water
  • Visit a doctor if fever lasts over 2 days
Immunity

Boost Your Immunity Naturally

  • Sleep 7–8 hours
  • Eat fruits, vegetables and protein
  • Exercise 30 minutes daily
  • Keep vaccinations up to date

General Health Awareness & Skin Care

Know more, worry less.

Awareness

Know Your Numbers: BP, Sugar and Weight

  • Check BP and sugar regularly after age 30
  • Reduce salt and sugar in daily food
  • Do an annual full body check-up
Allergy

Allergy Awareness: Causes, Signs and Care

  • Common triggers: dust, pollen, food, cosmetics, medicines
  • Signs: rashes, itching, sneezing, swelling
  • Always tell your doctor about past allergies
  • Do a patch test before any new skin product
Cosmetology

Why a Qualified Doctor Matters in Cosmetology & Aesthetics

  • Skin and hair treatments are medical procedures
  • A doctor checks allergies and skin type first
  • Prevents burns, infections and wrong products
  • Avoid unqualified salons for lasers, peels and injectables
Aesthetic

Safe Aesthetic Treatments: Know Before You Go

  • Ask about the procedure, risks and aftercare
  • Avoid sun exposure after peels and lasers
  • Never use leftover prescription creams
